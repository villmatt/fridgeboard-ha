# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Lights, switches, fans, scenes, scripts and sensors can be ticked, for the shared screen's new Smart home card. A tap on the card turns a light on or off, or runs a scene.
- A sensor's reading is sent to FridgeBoard at most once a minute.
- Your automations can now give a medicine, tick a job or a routine step, add to any list and put a message on the shared screens (see the Documentation tab).

## 0.1.0

- First version: sensors and buttons start walks, feed pets and tick chores; `fridgeboard_event` for your automations.
